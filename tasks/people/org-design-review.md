# Org Design Review

## Objetivo
Revisar o design organizacional para garantir que a estrutura suporta a estratégia, minimiza friction e maximiza velocity de execução. Org design errado é o killer silencioso — cria silos, duplica trabalho, gera conflitos e desacelera tudo.

## Agente Responsável
- **CHRO Agent** — Ownership de org design e people strategy

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CEO Agent** — Strategic alignment e final approval
- **COO Agent** — Operational effectiveness e cross-functional dependencies
- **CFO Agent** — Headcount budget e compensation structure
- **CTO Agent** — Engineering org design e team topologies

## Pré-requisitos
1. Current org chart with all positions and reporting lines
2. Strategic bets e OKRs (what the org needs to deliver)
3. Span of control data (direct reports per manager)
4. Collaboration patterns data (who works with whom)
5. Employee feedback on organizational friction
6. Headcount budget and constraints
7. Industry benchmarks for org structure at similar stage

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Current State Assessment (2-3 dias)
1. Map current org: all teams, reporting lines, headcount per team
2. Calculate span of control: average direct reports per manager (ideal: 5-9)
3. Identify layers: how many levels from CEO to individual contributor (ideal: 4-6)
4. Map decision-making authority: where are decisions made vs. where they should be
5. Identify collaboration patterns: cross-team dependencies, handoffs, bottlenecks
6. Collect employee feedback on org friction: surveys, 1:1s, exit interviews
7. Assess single points of failure: teams or roles that are critical with no backup

### Fase 2: Strategy-Structure Alignment (1-2 dias)
8. Map strategic bets to org structure: does each bet have a clear owner team?
9. Identify org gaps: strategic priorities without dedicated teams or owners
10. Identify org excess: teams or roles that don't map to current strategy
11. Evaluate team autonomy: can teams execute independently or are they constantly blocked?
12. Assess cognitive load per team: is each team's scope manageable?
13. Compare current structure with alternatives: functional, divisional, matrix, squad-based

### Fase 3: Redesign Proposals (2-3 dias)
14. Draft 2-3 org design alternatives with rationale for each
15. For each alternative: map reporting lines, span of control, decision authority
16. Evaluate each alternative against criteria: strategy alignment, speed, clarity, talent needs
17. Identify transition risks for each option: disruption, talent flight, execution pause
18. Calculate headcount and cost implications of each option
19. Select recommended option with CEO input
20. Plan implementation phases if redesign involves significant changes

### Fase 4: Communication e Implementation (1-2 semanas)
21. Prepare communication plan: who hears what, when, from whom
22. Brief affected leaders first (before public announcement)
23. Communicate to broader organization with context and rationale
24. Execute structural changes with HR support
25. Provide support for individuals in changed roles
26. Monitor transition: engagement, productivity, sentiment

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Conway's Law** — Org structure determines system architecture and vice versa
- **Team Topologies** — Stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-subsystem
- **Span of Control Guidelines** — 5-9 direct reports for effective management
- **Decision Rights Matrix (RAPID)** — Recommend, Agree, Perform, Input, Decide
- **Cognitive Load Theory** — Teams should own a manageable cognitive scope
- **Two-Pizza Teams** — Teams small enough to be fed by two pizzas

## Critérios de Aceitação
1. Current org fully documented and analyzed
2. Strategy-structure gaps identified
3. Design alternatives with pros/cons evaluated
4. Recommended design approved by CEO
5. Communication plan executed
6. Transition monitored for 90 days
7. 6-month review scheduled

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** Strategic Bets, Quarterly Planning, Employee Feedback, Exit Interview Data
- **Entrega para:** Exec Hiring, Headcount Planning, Team Formation, Budget Planning
- **Cadência:** Anual (full review) + Ad-hoc (triggered by strategy change or M&A)
- **Escalation path:** Org changes affecting >30% of company require board notification
- **Integração:** Feeds Exec Hiring, Succession Planning, Team Health Check
