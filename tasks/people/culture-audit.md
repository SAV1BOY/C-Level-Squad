# Culture Audit

## Objetivo
Avaliar a cultura organizacional atual, comparar com a cultura desejada e identificar gaps e ações para fortalecer ou corrigir. Cultura é o sistema operacional invisível da organização — define como decisões são tomadas, como conflitos são resolvidos e como pessoas se comportam quando ninguém está olhando.

## Agente Responsável
- **CHRO Agent** — Ownership de culture assessment e development

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CEO Agent** — Culture vision e tone from the top
- **COO Agent** — Operacional culture e process alignment
- **CTO Agent** — Engineering culture e technical values

## Pré-requisitos
1. Company values documentados
2. Employee engagement survey data (most recent)
3. Exit interview data (últimos 12 meses)
4. Glassdoor/employer brand data
5. Behavioral incident reports
6. Employee demographics e diversity data
7. Previous culture assessments (if any)

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Data Collection (2-3 semanas)
1. Deploy culture survey com questions em 5 dimensões: trust, accountability, innovation, collaboration, inclusion
2. Analyze existing engagement survey data for culture signals
3. Compile exit interview data: culture-related reasons for leaving
4. Review Glassdoor and employer brand sentiment
5. Conduct focus groups (4-6 groups of 8-10 people across levels and areas)
6. Interview 10-15 leaders individually about culture strengths and weaknesses
7. Observe rituals, meetings, and communication patterns for behavioral signals
8. Analyze decision-making patterns: who decides, how, how fast

### Fase 2: Culture Mapping (1-2 dias)
9. Map actual culture against stated values: where do behaviors match and diverge?
10. Identify dominant culture patterns: what are the unwritten rules?
11. Assess psychological safety: do people feel safe to speak up, disagree, fail?
12. Evaluate accountability culture: are commitments kept? Are there consequences?
13. Assess innovation culture: is experimentation encouraged and failure tolerated?
14. Evaluate inclusion: do all groups feel they belong and can contribute fully?
15. Classify culture type using Westrum model: pathological, bureaucratic, or generative

### Fase 3: Gap Analysis (1-2 dias)
16. Define desired culture: what culture do we need to execute our strategy?
17. Identify gaps between current and desired culture
18. Prioritize gaps by: strategic impact, severity, addressability
19. Root cause analysis for top gaps: why do these gaps exist?
20. Identify culture strengths to preserve and amplify
21. Benchmark against peer companies and best-in-class cultures

### Fase 4: Action Planning (1-2 dias)
22. Design culture initiatives for top 3 gaps
23. Identify "culture carriers" — people who exemplify desired behaviors
24. Propose ritual changes: meetings, communication, recognition that reinforce desired culture
25. Recommend leadership behavior changes (tone from the top)
26. Define culture metrics for ongoing tracking
27. Create culture improvement roadmap (quarterly milestones)
28. Present findings and plan to executive team

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Westrum Organizational Culture** — Pathological, Bureaucratic, Generative
- **Competing Values Framework (Quinn)** — Collaborate, Create, Control, Compete
- **Schein's Culture Model** — Artifacts, espoused values, underlying assumptions
- **Psychological Safety (Edmondson)** — Can people take interpersonal risks?
- **Culture Map (Meyer)** — Cross-cultural dimensions (if multi-cultural org)
- **Values-Behavior Alignment** — Do stated values match observed behaviors?

## Checklists de Qualidade
- [ ] Culture survey deployed with >70% response rate
- [ ] Focus groups conducted across levels and areas
- [ ] Leader interviews completed
- [ ] Exit interview data analyzed for culture signals
- [ ] Actual culture mapped against stated values
- [ ] Psychological safety assessed
- [ ] Inclusion assessed across demographics
- [ ] Desired culture defined and aligned with strategy
- [ ] Top 3 gaps prioritized with root cause
- [ ] Culture strengths identified for preservation
- [ ] Action plan with quarterly milestones
- [ ] Executive team aligned on findings and actions

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** Engagement Survey, Exit Interviews, Performance Review Data
- **Entrega para:** Leadership Development, Org Design, Values Refresh, Hiring Criteria
- **Cadência:** Annual (full audit) + Semi-annual (pulse check)
- **Escalation path:** Critical culture issues (harassment, ethical violations) escala imediately
- **Integração:** Feeds Team Health Check, Org Design Review, Performance Reviews
