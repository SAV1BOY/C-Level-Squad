# Performance Review Cycle

## Objetivo
Executar o ciclo de avaliação de desempenho para o executive team e garantir que os mesmos padrões cascateiam para toda a organização. Performance reviews bem feitas alinham expectativas, desenvolvem talento e tomam decisões difíceis sobre quem promover, desenvolver ou desligar.

## Agente Responsável
- **CHRO Agent** — Ownership do processo de performance review

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CEO Agent** — Avaliação direta dos C-Levels e calibração final
- **COO Agent** — Performance data operacional por área
- **CFO Agent** — Compensation review e budget para merit increases

## Pré-requisitos
1. OKR scores finalizados para o período de avaliação
2. Competency framework ou success profiles definidos por nível
3. Compensation benchmarks atualizados
4. Budget aprovado para merit increases, bonuses e promotions
5. Review templates padronizados
6. Calendar com deadlines publicado
7. Training para managers em como dar feedback efetivo

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Preparation (Semana 1-2)
1. Comunicar timeline, expectations e process para toda organização
2. Distribuir self-assessment templates para todos os colaboradores
3. Coletar peer feedback (360) para executives e managers
4. Compilar performance data: OKR scores, project outcomes, customer feedback
5. Preparar compensation analysis: current comp vs. market, internal equity
6. Train managers que são novos no processo (review writing, calibration)

### Fase 2: Individual Reviews (Semana 3-4)
7. Cada colaborador completa self-assessment
8. Manager prepara written review para cada direct report com:
   - Results assessment: o que foi entregue vs. esperado
   - Competency assessment: how vs. what (comportamentos e habilidades)
   - Development areas: onde precisa melhorar
   - Growth trajectory: ready now, ready next, needs development
9. Manager conduz 1:1 review conversation com cada report
10. Documentar feedback, ratings e development plan
11. Para C-Levels: CEO conduz reviews usando board feedback + OKR data + 360 input

### Fase 3: Calibration (Semana 5)
12. Calibration sessions por área: managers apresentam their ratings com evidência
13. Ensure consistency: same rating means same level of performance across teams
14. Identify top performers (top 10%) e underperformers (bottom 10%)
15. Discuss promotion candidates with evidence-based assessment
16. Discuss PIP (Performance Improvement Plan) candidates
17. CEO conducts executive calibration for C-Level team
18. Final ratings approved after calibration

### Fase 4: Compensation Decisions (Semana 6)
19. Map performance ratings to compensation actions (merit increase, bonus, equity refresh)
20. Ensure internal equity: similar performance = similar compensation
21. CFO validates total compensation spend within budget
22. Prepare offer letters for promotions and significant comp changes
23. Prepare communication for each manager to deliver to their team

### Fase 5: Communication e Development (Semana 7-8)
24. Managers communicate final ratings and compensation changes in 1:1s
25. Discuss e finalize development plans for next period
26. Process promotions formally (title change, compensation, announcement)
27. Initiate PIPs for underperformers with clear expectations and timeline
28. Analyze cycle outcomes: rating distribution, promotion rate, attrition risk
29. Document learnings for next cycle improvement

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Results + Behaviors Matrix** — Assess both what was delivered and how
- **9-Box Grid** — Performance vs. Potential for talent mapping
- **Calibration Protocol** — Forced distribution awareness, evidence-based ratings
- **Growth Trajectory Model** — Ready now, ready next, needs development
- **Radical Candor** — Care personally, challenge directly
- **SBI Feedback Model** — Situation, Behavior, Impact

## Checklists de Qualidade
- [ ] Timeline communicated to all with at least 4 weeks notice
- [ ] Self-assessments completed by all
- [ ] 360 feedback collected for executives and managers
- [ ] Performance data compiled (OKRs, outcomes)
- [ ] Written reviews completed for all
- [ ] 1:1 review conversations held
- [ ] Calibration sessions conducted per area
- [ ] Rating distribution reviewed for fairness
- [ ] Compensation actions within budget
- [ ] Internal equity verified
- [ ] Promotions processed
- [ ] PIPs initiated where needed
- [ ] Development plans documented

## Critérios de Aceitação
1. 100% of employees reviewed within the cycle
2. Calibration completed for all areas
3. Rating distribution within expected ranges
4. Compensation actions within approved budget
5. All reviews communicated in 1:1 conversations
6. Development plans documented for all
7. PIPs initiated for underperformers with clear criteria

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** OKR Scores, 360 Feedback, Compensation Benchmarks, Budget Approval
- **Entrega para:** Compensation Processing, Promotion Announcements, Development Programs, PIPs
- **Cadência:** Semi-annual (full cycle) + Quarterly (check-ins)
- **Timeline:** 8 weeks end-to-end
- **Escalation path:** Disputes escalate to skip-level manager, then CHRO
