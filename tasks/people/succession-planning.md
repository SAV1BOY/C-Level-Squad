# Succession Planning

## Objetivo
Garantir que a organização tem planos de sucessão para todas as posições críticas, minimizando risco de disrupção quando líderes saem (planejado ou não). Sem succession planning, a saída de um líder chave pode paralisar uma área inteira por meses.

## Agente Responsável
- **CHRO Agent** — Ownership do processo de succession planning

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CEO Agent** — Succession para C-Level positions e board involvement
- **COO Agent** — Operational continuity requirements
- **CFO Agent** — Compensation planning para successors

## Pré-requisitos
1. Org chart atualizado com todas as posições críticas
2. Performance review data (últimos 2 ciclos)
3. 9-Box grid ou talent assessment recente
4. Career aspirations data (from 1:1s e development conversations)
5. External talent pipeline data
6. Competency frameworks por nível
7. Board guidance para CEO succession (se aplicável)

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Critical Role Identification (1-2 dias)
1. Identify all critical roles: C-Level, VPs, Directors, and key individual contributors
2. Define "critical" criteria: strategic importance, unique knowledge, difficulty to replace
3. Assess vacancy risk for each critical role: likelihood of departure (Low/Med/High)
4. Calculate business impact of vacancy for each role (revenue, customer, operational)
5. Prioritize roles for succession planning by: impact × vacancy risk

### Fase 2: Successor Assessment (2-3 dias)
6. For each critical role, identify internal successor candidates (0-3 per role)
7. Classify each candidate: ready now, ready in 1-2 years, ready in 3+ years
8. Assess gaps between current capability and role requirements for each candidate
9. Create development plan to close gaps for top candidates
10. Identify roles with NO internal successors (external dependency)
11. For roles with no internal candidates, define external sourcing strategy
12. Assess bench strength by area: which areas have strong pipeline vs. thin bench

### Fase 3: Development Planning (1-2 dias)
13. For "ready now" candidates: ensure they have exposure to the full scope of the role
14. For "ready in 1-2 years": create specific development assignments, mentoring, training
15. For "ready in 3+" candidates: longer-term development path with milestones
16. Consider stretch assignments, rotations, and special projects as development tools
17. Ensure diversity in successor pipeline
18. Budget development investments per high-potential candidate

### Fase 4: Emergency Succession (1 dia)
19. Create emergency succession plan: who steps in immediately if someone leaves tomorrow
20. For each critical role, define interim coverage plan
21. Ensure key knowledge is documented (not trapped in one person's head)
22. Define communication protocol for unplanned departures
23. Review and update emergency plans quarterly

### Fase 5: Review e Governance (ongoing)
24. Present succession status to CEO and board (for C-Level roles)
25. Track development progress for successor candidates quarterly
26. Update plans when people move, are promoted, or leave
27. Annual full refresh of succession plans

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **9-Box Grid** — Performance vs. Potential for talent mapping
- **Readiness Assessment** — Ready now, ready soon, ready later, not ready
- **Development Velocity** — How fast candidates are progressing
- **Bench Strength Index** — Ratio of ready successors to critical roles
- **Emergency Succession Protocol** — Immediate coverage plans
- **Knowledge Transfer Framework** — Ensuring critical knowledge is shared

## Checklists de Qualidade
- [ ] All critical roles identified and prioritized
- [ ] Vacancy risk assessed for each role
- [ ] Internal successor candidates identified (0-3 per role)
- [ ] Readiness level classified for each candidate
- [ ] Development plans created for top candidates
- [ ] Roles with no internal successors flagged
- [ ] Emergency succession plans for all C-Level roles
- [ ] Key knowledge documented and not single-person dependent
- [ ] Diversity of successor pipeline assessed
- [ ] Development budgets allocated
- [ ] Board briefed on C-Level succession (if applicable)

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** Performance Reviews, 9-Box Assessments, Org Design Review
- **Entrega para:** Development Programs, Exec Hiring (for gaps), Board Reporting
- **Cadência:** Annual (full review) + Quarterly (progress check) + Triggered (by departures)
- **Escalation path:** Critical role with no successor and high vacancy risk escala to CEO
- **Integração:** Feeds Exec Hiring, Performance Review, Board Prep
