# Executive Hiring Process

## Objetivo
Executar o processo completo de contratação executiva (C-Level, VP, Director), garantindo que a organização atrai, avalia e contrata líderes excepcionais. Contratar o executivo errado é um dos erros mais caros — impacta cultura, estratégia e resultados por trimestres ou anos até ser corrigido.

## Agente Responsável
- **CHRO Agent** — Ownership do processo de hiring e people operations

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CEO Agent** — Decisão final e hiring manager para C-Level positions
- **COO Agent** — Operational requirements e performance expectations
- **CFO Agent** — Compensation benchmarking e budget approval
- **CTO Agent** — Technical assessment para engineering leadership roles
- **Hiring Manager** — O líder direto da posição (varia conforme o role)

## Pré-requisitos
1. Org design review indicando necessidade da posição (ver `tasks/people/org-design-review.md`)
2. Headcount aprovado no budget
3. Role scorecard definido (outcomes esperados, competencies, cultural fit)
4. Compensation benchmark para o nível e mercado
5. Interview panel definido
6. Search strategy definida: internal promotion, external search, executive search firm

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Role Definition (Semana 1)
1. Criar role scorecard: missão do role, 3-5 outcomes esperados para 12 meses, competencies
2. Definir "must-haves" vs. "nice-to-haves" — ser brutalmente honesto sobre o que é essencial
3. Documentar culture add criteria: como este líder deve complementar o time existente
4. Definir compensation range: base, equity, bonus, benefits (benchmarked)
5. Decidir search strategy: internal-only, external, ou hybrid
6. Se external search: selecionar e engajar executive search firm (se necessário)
7. Criar candidate persona: background, trajectory, motivations do candidato ideal

### Fase 2: Sourcing (Semana 2-4)
8. Ativar network do executive team: cada C-Level indica 3-5 candidatos potenciais
9. Se using search firm: brief a firma com scorecard e candidate persona
10. Mapear candidatos internos que poderiam ser promovidos (succession planning alignment)
11. Source diretamente: LinkedIn, eventos, comunidades, back-channel references
12. Target list: criar lista de 15-20 candidatos potenciais
13. Outreach personalizado para cada target (não template genérico)
14. Meta: 8-10 candidates em pipeline ativo

### Fase 3: Assessment (Semana 3-6)
15. **Screen (30 min):** Fit check rápido — motivação, alignment cultural, salary expectations
16. **Deep Dive (60 min):** Hiring manager interview — past achievements, strategic thinking, leadership style
17. **Case Study (60 min):** Cenário real da empresa para avaliar problem-solving e decisão
18. **Panel Interview (60 min):** Cross-functional panel com 3-4 futuros peers
19. **Reference Checks (back-channel):** 4-6 referências não fornecidas pelo candidato
20. **Culture Interview (45 min):** CEO ou CHRO avalia culture fit e values alignment
21. Calibração: panel discussion para comparar top 2-3 candidatos
22. Para C-Level: board members podem querer entrevistar (arranjar se necessário)

### Fase 4: Closing (Semana 6-8)
23. Preparar offer package: comp total, equity, start date, expectations doc
24. CEO (ou hiring manager) faz offer call pessoalmente — não por email
25. Negociar termos dentro do range aprovado
26. Close: celebrar e preparar onboarding plan
27. Comunicar contratação internamente com contexto e entusiasmo
28. Comunicar externamente se appropriate (press release, LinkedIn)

### Fase 5: Onboarding (Primeiros 90 dias)
29. Preparar 30-60-90 day plan com milestones claros
30. Schedule 1:1s com todos os peers (C-Level) na primeira semana
31. Provide "state of the company" briefing com documentos chave
32. Assign executive buddy para primeiros 90 dias
33. Schedule 30-day, 60-day e 90-day check-ins com CEO/hiring manager
34. Avaliar performance ao final dos 90 dias com feedback 360

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Scorecard Method (Who by Smart/Street)** — Outcomes-based role definition
- **Topgrading** — Structured behavioral interviews with career chronology
- **Culture Add (not Culture Fit)** — Seek complementary, not identical profiles
- **Reference Back-Channel** — References the candidate didn't provide
- **30-60-90 Day Plan** — Structured onboarding with clear milestones
- **A-Player Assessment** — Would we enthusiastically re-hire this person?

## Checklists de Qualidade
- [ ] Role scorecard with 3-5 outcomes defined
- [ ] Compensation benchmarked and range approved
- [ ] Search strategy selected and activated
- [ ] 15-20 target candidates identified
- [ ] 8-10 candidates in active pipeline
- [ ] Structured interview process followed (same process for all)
- [ ] Case study or work sample included
- [ ] 4-6 back-channel references completed
- [ ] Panel calibration session conducted
- [ ] Offer competitive and aligned with benchmarks
- [ ] 30-60-90 day onboarding plan prepared
- [ ] 90-day check-in scheduled

## Critérios de Aceitação
1. Role scorecard defined with measurable outcomes
2. Structured interview process followed consistently
3. Minimum 4 back-channel references completed for finalist
4. Panel calibration conducted with documented rationale
5. Offer within approved compensation range
6. 30-60-90 day onboarding plan in place before start date
7. 90-day review completed

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** Org Design Review, Succession Planning, Budget Approval
- **Entrega para:** Onboarding, Team Communication, Org Chart Update
- **Cadência:** As needed (triggered by vacancy or new position)
- **Timeline típico:** 6-10 weeks from search start to offer acceptance
- **Escalation path:** C-Level hiring requires CEO and potentially board involvement
