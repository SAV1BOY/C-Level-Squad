# Stakeholder Communication Review

## Objetivo
Avaliar a qualidade e eficácia da comunicação com todos os stakeholders (board, investors, employees, customers, partners), identificando gaps e melhorando a transparência e alinhamento. Comunicação ruim é a raiz de muitos problemas organizacionais — expectativas desalinhadas, surpresas evitáveis e perda de confiança.

## Agente Responsável
- **COO Agent** — Consolidação e facilitação do review

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CEO Agent** — Board e investor communication quality
- **CMO Agent** — External communication e brand consistency
- **CHRO Agent** — Employee communication e engagement
- **CPO Agent** — Customer communication e product updates
- **CFO Agent** — Financial reporting e investor relations

## Pré-requisitos
1. Communication log (all major communications in the period)
2. Stakeholder feedback data (surveys, sentiment)
3. Board meeting feedback
4. Employee engagement/communication survey data
5. Customer communication metrics (NPS, satisfaction)
6. Media coverage and PR data
7. Internal communication analytics (email opens, intranet engagement)

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Stakeholder Mapping (1 dia)
1. List all stakeholder groups: board, investors, employees, customers, partners, regulators, media
2. For each group, define: communication needs, frequency expectations, preferred channels
3. Identify communication owners for each stakeholder group
4. Map current communication cadences vs. stakeholder expectations
5. Identify stakeholders that are under-communicated or over-communicated

### Fase 2: Communication Quality Assessment (2-3 dias)
6. For each stakeholder group, assess communication quality:
   - **Timeliness:** Do they receive information when they need it?
   - **Clarity:** Is the message clear and actionable?
   - **Consistency:** Is messaging consistent across channels and spokespersons?
   - **Transparency:** Are we sharing the right level of detail (including bad news)?
   - **Two-way:** Can stakeholders provide feedback and are they heard?
7. Collect feedback from each stakeholder group (survey, interviews, proxy data)
8. Assess employee communication: are all-hands, updates, and changes well communicated?
9. Assess board communication: are board packages complete, timely, and honest?
10. Assess customer communication: are product changes, issues, and updates well communicated?

### Fase 3: Gap Analysis (1-2 dias)
11. Identify communication gaps by stakeholder group
12. Identify instances where lack of communication caused problems (surprises, misalignment)
13. Assess communication during crises: were we fast, transparent, and empathetic?
14. Evaluate consistency: are different people saying different things about the same topic?
15. Identify communication channels that are underused or ineffective
16. Benchmark against best practices for each stakeholder type

### Fase 4: Improvement Plan (1 dia)
17. Propose communication calendar improvements per stakeholder group
18. Recommend communication channel optimization
19. Define communication standards: templates, tone, approval workflow
20. Propose crisis communication protocol improvements
21. Define communication effectiveness metrics for ongoing tracking
22. Present findings and recommendations to executive team

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Stakeholder Mapping (Power/Interest)** — Manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, monitor
- **Communication Effectiveness Model** — Timeliness, clarity, consistency, transparency, two-way
- **Radical Transparency** — Share context and reasoning, not just decisions
- **Crisis Communication Protocol** — Speed, honesty, empathy, action plan
- **Internal Communication Best Practices** — Cascade model, multi-channel, feedback loops
- **Minto Pyramid** — Conclusion first, then supporting points
