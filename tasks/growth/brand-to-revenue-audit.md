# Brand to Revenue Audit

## Objetivo
Auditar a conexão entre investimentos em brand e geração de receita, validando se os esforços de marca estão contribuindo para o funil de growth. Brand sem connection com revenue é vaidade; revenue sem brand é frágil. Este audit encontra e fortalece o link entre os dois.

## Agente Responsável
- **CMO Agent** — Ownership da análise brand-to-revenue

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CFO Agent** — Financial validation e revenue attribution
- **CPO Agent** — Product experience como extensão da marca
- **CEO Agent** — Brand vision e strategic positioning
- **COO Agent** — Customer experience alignment

## Pré-requisitos
1. Brand metrics atualizados: awareness, consideration, preference, NPS
2. Revenue data segmentado por cohort e channel
3. Customer survey data sobre brand perception
4. Marketing spend breakdown: brand vs. performance
5. Competitive brand positioning data
6. Win/loss data com brand mentions
7. Pricing power analysis (ability to charge premium)

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Brand Health Assessment (2-3 dias)
1. Compilar brand health metrics: aided/unaided awareness, consideration set, preference
2. Medir Net Promoter Score e trend (quarterly view)
3. Analisar brand sentiment em canais públicos (social, reviews, press)
4. Executar competitive brand perception comparison
5. Avaliar brand consistency: messaging, visual, tone across touchpoints
6. Medir share of voice vs. competitors
7. Calcular brand investment as % of total marketing spend

### Fase 2: Revenue Connection Analysis (2-3 dias)
8. Correlacionar brand metrics com revenue metrics (lagged correlation analysis)
9. Analisar impact de brand awareness em conversion rates (brand search vs non-brand)
10. Calcular brand premium: quanto mais clientes pagam vs. commoditized alternative
11. Avaliar brand impact em sales cycle: deals com brand awareness vs. cold outreach
12. Analisar customer acquisition cost por brand awareness level
13. Medir brand contribution to expansion revenue: upsell rates por brand affinity
14. Calcular brand contribution to retention: churn rate por brand loyalty score

### Fase 3: Gap Identification (1-2 dias)
15. Identificar brand-revenue disconnects: onde brand invest não gera revenue return
16. Mapear brand touchpoints que mais influenciam purchase decision
17. Identificar brand gaps vs. competitors que impactam win rate
18. Avaliar brand as hiring tool: impact em talent acquisition e employer brand
19. Identificar brand assets underutilized (content, thought leadership, community)
20. Mapear brand risk factors: dependency em single spokesperson, PR vulnerabilities

### Fase 4: Action Plan e Recommendations (1 dia)
21. Propor brand investment adjustments baseado em revenue impact data
22. Recomendar brand initiatives com highest revenue connection potential
23. Definir brand KPIs ligados a revenue outcomes
24. Criar measurement framework para tracking contínuo
25. Apresentar findings para executive team

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Brand Funnel** — Awareness → Consideration → Preference → Purchase → Loyalty
- **Brand Equity Model (Aaker)** — Awareness, associations, perceived quality, loyalty
- **Brand-Revenue Correlation** — Lagged correlation entre brand metrics e revenue
- **Share of Voice / Share of Market** — SOV-SOM analysis para growth prediction
- **Price Premium Analysis** — Brand contribution to pricing power
- **Customer Journey Brand Mapping** — Brand touchpoints que mais influenciam decisão

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** Brand Survey Data, Revenue Analytics, Competitive Intelligence
- **Entrega para:** Marketing Strategy, Brand Investment Decisions, Annual Planning
- **Cadência:** Semestral (deep audit) + Trimestral (metrics check)
- **Escalation path:** Brand decline sustained for 2+ quarters escala para CEO
- **Integração:** Alimenta Marketing ROI Review e Annual Planning
