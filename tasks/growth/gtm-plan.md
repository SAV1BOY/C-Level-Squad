# Create Go-to-Market Plan

## Objetivo
Criar um plano de Go-to-Market (GTM) completo para um novo produto, feature significativa ou entrada em novo segmento. O GTM plan define como levaremos a oferta ao mercado de forma coordenada entre produto, marketing, vendas e customer success, maximizando impacto e minimizando friction na adoção.

## Agente Responsável
- **CMO Agent** — Ownership do GTM plan e coordenação cross-functional

## Agentes de Suporte
- **CPO Agent** — Product positioning, feature readiness e customer feedback
- **CEO Agent** — Strategic alignment e pricing approval
- **CFO Agent** — Financial modeling, pricing strategy e ROI projections
- **CTO Agent** — Technical readiness e scalability
- **COO Agent** — Operational readiness e capacity planning
- **CHRO Agent** — Sales hiring e training readiness

## Pré-requisitos
1. Produto ou feature definido e em desenvolvimento (ou pronto)
2. Target customer profile (ICP) documentado
3. Market thesis validada para o segmento alvo
4. Competitive landscape analysis do espaço específico
5. Pricing hypothesis ou framework definido
6. Sales e CS capacity assessment
7. Budget aprovado para go-to-market activities

## Processo (step-by-step)

### Fase 1: Foundation (Semana 1)
1. Definir o escopo do GTM: novo produto, nova feature, novo mercado ou expansion
2. Articular a value proposition clara: por que o cliente deve comprar/adotar isso
3. Definir o Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) com firmographics e behavioral triggers
4. Mapear a buyer journey: awareness → consideration → decision → adoption → expansion
5. Documentar competitive alternatives: o que o cliente faz hoje sem nosso produto
6. Definir success metrics do GTM: acquisition, activation, revenue targets

### Fase 2: Strategy (Semana 2)
7. Definir pricing strategy: modelo, tiers, packaging, discounting rules
8. Escolher go-to-market motion: product-led, sales-led, community-led ou hybrid
9. Definir channel strategy: direct sales, partnerships, marketplaces, self-serve
10. Criar messaging framework: headline, sub-headlines, proof points, objection handling
11. Definir launch tier: big bang (full launch), phased (segment by segment), ou soft launch
12. Planejar demand generation: inbound, outbound, events, partnerships, PR
13. Definir enablement needs: sales training, demo environments, battle cards

### Fase 3: Execution Planning (Semana 3)
14. Criar timeline detalhado com milestones de launch (T-30, T-14, T-7, T-0, T+7, T+30)
15. Definir pre-launch activities: beta program, early access, analyst briefings
16. Criar content plan: landing pages, blog posts, case studies, videos, webinars
17. Preparar sales enablement package: pitch deck, demo script, objection handlers, ROI calculator
18. Definir customer success readiness: onboarding playbook, support documentation
19. Planejar internal launch: all-hands, team training, FAQ for customer-facing teams
20. Preparar PR e analyst relations plan (se aplicável)

### Fase 4: Launch Execution (Semana 4+)
21. Executar pre-launch checklist: tudo pronto antes de go-live
22. Coordinar launch day activities: website update, emails, social, PR
23. Ativar demand generation campaigns conforme plano
24. Monitorar early signals: website traffic, sign-ups, demo requests, pipeline created
25. Coletar early customer feedback e iterar rapidamente
26. Executar post-launch review (T+7 e T+30) com métricas vs targets

## Frameworks a Aplicar
- **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)** — Ancorar messaging no job que o cliente está contratando
- **Crossing the Chasm (Moore)** — Identificar o segmento beachhead e strategy
- **AIDA Framework** — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action para messaging
- **STP (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning)** — Clareza de quem e como
- **Pricing Psychology** — Anchoring, decoy, value-based pricing
- **Pirate Metrics (AARRR)** — Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, Referral

- [ ] Buyer journey mapeada com touchpoints

- [ ] Sales enablement package pronto (deck, demo, objection handlers)

- [ ] Post-launch review agendado (T+7 e T+30)

## Critérios de Aceitação
1. GTM plan completo com todos os componentes documentados
2. Pricing aprovado pelo CEO e CFO
3. Sales enablement package pronto e validado
4. Launch timeline com owners para cada milestone
5. Success metrics definidos com targets mensuráveis
6. Budget aprovado e alocado
7. Post-launch review executado (T+7 e T+30)

## Dependências e Handoffs
- **Recebe de:** Product Roadmap (CPO), Market Thesis (CEO), Pricing Analysis (CFO)
- **Entrega para:** Sales Team, Marketing Execution, Customer Success, Product (feedback loop)
- **Cadência:** Per-launch (cada novo produto ou feature significativa)
- **Timeline típico:** 4-6 semanas de planejamento, execution ongoing
- **Escalation path:** Readiness gaps que ameaçam launch date escalam para COO
